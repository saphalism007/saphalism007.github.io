# Saphal's Dictionary

An advanced English dictionary written for a Nepali speaker learning to sound
American. Every word carries its part of speech, its pronunciation written in
Nepali Devanagari the way an American actually says it, the meaning in plain
English, the meaning in Nepali, and an example sentence.

- **1000 advanced and SAT level words**
- **1000 idioms** and **1000 phrases**, each with a meaning and a sentence
- Save words, mark them learned, review them on a spaced schedule, and take quizzes
- An A to Z rail on every list for fast jumping

Open it here: **https://saphalism007.github.io**

## Install it as an app

It is a normal web page, so there is nothing to download and nothing to pay for.

- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, then tap the menu and choose *Install app*.
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Computer:** open the link in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar.

Once installed it opens fullscreen with its own icon and works with no internet.

## Privacy

Nothing is collected and nothing is sent anywhere. Your saved words and quiz
progress are stored only in your own browser, on your own device.

## How this site is built

The whole app is one file, `index.html`, generated from the source in the
`saphals-dictionary` project. Rebuild and publish it with `npm run deploy`.
